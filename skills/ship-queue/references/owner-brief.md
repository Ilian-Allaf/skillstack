# The owner's brief

What each owner of a queue gets, and nothing of your own reasoning. Fill every line.

- **The goal**, in the user's words, and this task in one line, with its issue number when it is one: `ship` claims and links it.
- **The base**: the repo's base branch, or the branch of the PR this task builds on.
- **The skill**: the path of `ship/SKILL.md`, to follow from step 1 to step 8.
- **The paths it may write**, and those it must not.
- **The resume note**, when the queue was paused: its path, to resume through `resume-task`.
- **The rules**, word for word:
  - "Never merge, and never push to another owner's branch."
  - "Push your branch after the frame and after each build attempt, so the coordinator sees your work."
  - "Babysit only your own PR. When your PR is stacked on another task's, rebase your own branch on its new head, and report to the coordinator what the branch below needs."
  - "When you cannot spawn your own verifier, say so: the coordinator spawns it."
