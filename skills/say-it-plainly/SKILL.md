# Say it plainly

Read [the constitution](../constitution/README.md) first.

## Steps

1. **Restate your last reply.** Keep every fact and every decision waiting on the user. Drop the jargon, the identifiers the user does not need, and the hedges. Short sentences, said the way one person tells another. Name each thing the way the user would.
   Done when: the restatement is shorter than the original, and a reader new to the work can act on it.

## Reply

The restatement only.
