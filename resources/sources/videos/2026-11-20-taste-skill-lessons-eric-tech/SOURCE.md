## Collection and reliability

Title, channel, publication date, duration, description and chapters were retrieved through `yt-dlp`. English automatic captions were retrieved through `youtube-transcript-api` with [`tools/fetch_youtube_transcript.py`](../../../tools/fetch_youtube_transcript.py), the same collector as the other videos.

Automatic captions misrecognize names: "Mobin" is likely Mobbin, "Paul Bakos" and "GPT-6.1 Soul" are unchecked. Check names and quotations against the video before citing them. Star counts, run lengths (45 hours, 55 subagents) and model names are the presenter's reports, not facts checked here.

## What kind of source this is

A secondary source. The eight lessons condense an article by Leon Lin, author of the Taste Skill, published on X on 2026-10-31 ("How I prompt AI to build good-looking demos", 11 tips). Lesson 6 is the presenter's own addition, and so are the tool suggestions beyond Mobbin and X (Dribbble, Inspo MCP, agent-browser, Impeccable). The video also promotes the presenter's community and courses.

The primary material it points to was not imported:

- Leon Lin's article: <https://x.com/LexnLin/status/2116662591363690999>
- Taste Skill: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill>
- Cook skill: <https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cook>
- Impeccable, the slop detector it recommends (named, not linked, in the video)

Attribute the lessons to Leon Lin through this video, lesson 6 and the tool picks to Eric Tech. Before a framework decision rests on one of these claims, read the primary source.
