## Collection and reliability

Title and channel were checked through YouTube oEmbed. Publication date, duration and chapters were retrieved through `yt-dlp`. English automatic captions were retrieved through `youtube-transcript-api`. The import retains the timed segments in `raw/segments.json` and a readable transcript in `transcript.md`, using the same collector as the Lauren Tan video. Reading notes and analysis are separate from the extracted captions.

Automatic captions can misrecognize names and product terminology. Check quotations against the video before publication. Timestamps in the notes refer to the video's chapter boundaries. Demonstrations, cost estimates and productivity claims are the presenter's reports, not measurements reproduced in this repository.

The description explicitly links [Addy Osmani's article](../../articles/2026-11-16-claude-code-verification/SOURCE.md). Attribute the article's process claims to Osmani and Amjad's sandbox examples to Amjad; the video is not independent corroboration of the article.
